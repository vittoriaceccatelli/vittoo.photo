Photography Opinions

Opinions

Books

Reading Lolita in Tehran
Azar Nafisi · 2003
One of the most powerful books I've read about the quiet, stubborn act of reading as resistance. The way Nafisi weaves literature into lived experience under the Islamic Republic so naturally stays with you.
Educated
Tara Westover · 2018
Westover's account of building an identity from scratch and questioning everything you have been brought up to believe is extraordinary. The power, ambition, self-awareness and empathy is unlike anything else I've read.
A Brief History of Time
Stephen Hawking · 1988
A book that makes you feel so small and insignificant in the best way. You finish reading this and start questioning everything you thought you knew.
Prisoners of Geography
Tim Marshall · 2015
A compelling and relevant exploration of how geographical features have shaped and continue to influence global events and political outcomes everywhere.
How to Avoid a Climate Disaster
Bill Gates · 2021
Unusually practical and optimistic book on climate: less about catastrophe, more about what needs to and can happen.
Vom Ende der Einsamkeit
Benedict Wells · 2016
The most beautiful German novel I've read. Wells writes about grief and time in a very moving way.

Podcasts

The Rest Is Politics
Alastair Campbell & Rory Stewart
The only political podcast where the hosts regularly disagree and admit what they don't know, without ending in heated arguments. Campbell and Stewart make politics still feel intellectual, inclusive and backed by evidence.
The Rest Is Politics: Leading
Alastair Campbell & Rory Stewart
The longer-form conversations revolve more around thinking about how power actually works, the philosophical underpinnings of political decision-making, and all other aspects even more distantly related to politics.

Film

Hamnet
Chloé Zhao · 2025
Zhao keeps the grief at the centre. The way the movie handles the relationship between loss and life feels exactly right.
Life Is Beautiful
Roberto Benigni · 1997
A movie on the power of hope and imagination in the face of the most difficult, tragic and devastating circumstances.
The Pianist
Roman Polanski · 2002
Brody carries the whole film almost without dialogue. The survival it depicts is unglamorous, which is exactly why it stays with you.
Memento
Christopher Nolan · 2000
The structure of this movie puts you exactly in the position of the character. One of the few films where form and content are inseparable.
Good Will Hunting
Gus Van Sant · 1997
The resistance, breakthroughs, honesty, anger, and empathy in the conversations between the two leads are moving.
Notting Hill
Roger Michell · 1999
The best romantic comedy.
Oppenheimer
Christopher Nolan · 2023
The intercutting of the trial with the Manhattan Project is structurally brilliant. It captures the complexity of the moral choices involved.
The Talented Mr. Ripley
Anthony Minghella · 1999
A film about wanting to be someone else so badly it destroys you.
Marty Supreme
Jonah Hill · 2025
Jonah Hill turns ping pong into something genuinely cinematic. Really about what it costs to be the best at something nobody else takes seriously.
Perfect Days
Wim Wenders · 2023
A quiet, precise film about finding beauty in routine. Wenders and Yakusho show that the most meaningful lives are sometimes the smallest ones.

Artists

Robert Doisneau
1912 – 1994 · French
The warmth in his street photography is beautiful. He makes Paris feel like a place with so much character and life.
Edward Burtynsky
b. 1955 · Canadian
The scale of his landscapes forces you to reckon with our environment and the consequences of the actions of humanity in a beautiful way.
Ansel Adams
1902 – 1984 · American
Ansel Adams' landscapes were pioneering and show the beauty of the American West.
Henri Cartier-Bresson
1908 – 2004 · French
His instinct for geometry and timing is remarking.
Dorothea Lange
1895 – 1965 · American
Her Depression-era work turned documentary photography into moral witness.
Francis Bacon
1909 – 1992 · Irish-British
Difficult to sit with. The distortion is at the same time extremely beautiful and unsettling.
