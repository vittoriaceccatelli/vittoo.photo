Building

Kindred
Why

Most recommendation engines optimise for engagement, not taste. I wanted something that actually learns what I love — not what I click on, but what I finish, reread, or abandon halfway through.

How

A swipe interface for rating books you've already read. Each rating feeds an embedding model that builds a taste profile, layered with collaborative filtering to surface books you haven't encountered yet.

What's next

Cross-modal extension to films. The same representation space that maps books by content should, in principle, map films — a single taste graph across media types.
